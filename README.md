# What Should I Post Today?

A simple web app that helps content creators find new ideas for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram.

## Features

- Random content idea generator
- Ideas for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram
- Fast and easy to use
- Mobile-friendly
- Free and lightweight

## How It Works

1. Open the website.
2. Click the **Generate Idea** button.
3. Get a random content idea instantly.
4. Create your post and grow your audience.

## Example Ideas

- Show your daily routine.
- Reveal a hidden talent.
- Rate your favorite snacks.
- Share your biggest goal.
- Show your gaming setup.
- Try a trending challenge.
